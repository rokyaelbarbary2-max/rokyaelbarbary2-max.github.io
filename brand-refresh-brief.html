Eight Questions to Answer Before a Brand Refresh

A brand refresh goes wrong when the brief says "make it modern" and nothing else. Before anyone opens a design tool, answer these eight questions in writing.

  1. Why now? A new market, a new audience, a merger or a product change are good reasons. "We are bored of the logo" is not.
  2. Who must it work for? Name the two or three audiences, including the language they read in.
  3. What stays? List the assets customers already recognise, such as a colour, a name or a shape.
  4. What must change? Be specific: legibility at small sizes, clashes with competitors, no Arabic version.
  5. Where will it appear? Signage, packaging, social avatars, invoices, a website header and a favicon all have different limits.
  6. How will you judge it? Agree the criteria first, such as recognisability in a five-second test.
  7. Who decides? One named person, with a short list of reviewers.
  8. What is the rollout plan? Decide which materials change first and what happens to old ones.
