Free marketing and SEO tools for small teams

Small teams rarely need a large marketing stack. A handful of free tools, used consistently, covers most of the early work: finding what people search for, checking that pages can be crawled, measuring speed and tracking which pages bring in enquiries.

What to look for in a free tool

Pick tools that do one job clearly, that show their sources, and that you will genuinely open every month. A tool you never open is worse than a spreadsheet you maintain.

A simple monthly routine

  1. Review search queries and page performance in Search Console.
  2. Run a crawl and fix broken links, missing titles and duplicate descriptions.
  3. Test your key pages on mobile and fix the biggest speed issues first.
  4. Check structured data on your service and contact pages.
  5. Write down three questions customers asked, then answer them on a real page.
